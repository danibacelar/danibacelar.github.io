What's included

Built around your child's actual coursework.

Review of school content

We go over what's being taught in class right now, using the same vocabulary and structures from your child's textbook.

Homework support

Working through assignments together, so your child understands the "why," not just the answer.

Assessment preparation

Targeted review before tests and quizzes, focused on the specific concepts a student finds difficult.

Practice beyond the lesson

Interactive games, built for what your child is struggling with.

When a student is having a hard time with a specific concept — prepositions, past tense, a vocabulary set — Mrs. Dani builds an interactive activity around exactly that, using the vocabulary from their own textbook where possible.
