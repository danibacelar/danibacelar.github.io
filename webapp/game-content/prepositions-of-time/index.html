⏰ IN, ON, AT — Prepositions of Time

Learn the rules, then practice!

📘 Language Focus

We use different prepositions to talk about time. Look at the rules below:

AT

Exact clock times & a few special expressions

  • at 9 o'clock
  • at night
  • at lunchtime
  • at the weekend

IN

Longer periods: months, years, seasons, parts of the day

  • in August
  • in winter
  • in 2024
  • in the morning

ON

Days and dates

  • on Sunday
  • on Monday morning
  • on my birthday
  • on July 5th
💡 Memory trick: AT = one exact point on the clock ⏱️ · IN = a long period, like being "inside" a box of time 📦 · ON = a specific day, like a date "on" the calendar 📅

Now look at the poem from your book — can you spot the pattern?

In winter at nine at night, I'm in bed because it's too dark. In August at nine at night, I'm still playing in the park! On Sunday at eight in the morning, I go out for a long run. On Monday at six in the evening, I meet up with friends for some fun.
